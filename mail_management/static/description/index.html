Mail Management

Full webmail inside Odoo, with an AI assistant

Mail Management brings a complete webmail client into the Odoo backend. Connect Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail or any IMAP/SMTP mailbox, work with folders, read and compose messages, and ask an AI assistant to summarize threads, triage the inbox or draft replies.

Features

What it does

Three-pane webmail

Folder tree, message list and reading pane, plus a rich compose window built on Odoo's HTML editor.

Your own mailboxes

Presets for Gmail and Outlook/Hotmail/Live, or manual IMAP/SMTP for any provider. Folders are discovered and synced automatically.

Encrypted credentials

Mailbox passwords and AI keys are stored encrypted, never in plain text.

AI assistant

Chat about your mailbox, summarize threads with citations, triage the inbox and draft replies.

Signatures

Create signatures with a built-in generator and choose where they go in replies.

Personal settings

Theme, remote-image blocking, quoted-text collapse, read and delivery receipts, spellcheck and more.

Getting started

How it works

1
Install
Python dependencies: cryptography and imap_tools.
2
Add a mailbox
Choose Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail/Live or manual setup, then test the connection.
3
Open the webmail
Folders are discovered and kept in sync by a scheduled action.
4
Optional: connect AI
Pick Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and enter the key.

Details

Feature Detail
AI providers Google Gemini · OpenAI · Anthropic Claude · DeepSeek · custom OpenAI-compatible
AI safety The assistant never sends email. Drafts always need your review, and instructions inside emails are ignored
Trash retention Configurable automatic purge of the trash
Reading Remote images blocked by default, quoted text collapsed, HTML or plain text
Composing HTML editor, signatures, read and delivery receipts, spellcheck
